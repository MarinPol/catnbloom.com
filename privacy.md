**Last updated:** August 3, 2026

CatnBloom Studio ("CatnBloom," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through catnbloom.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices available to you. 

## 1. Who We Are

CatnBloom Studio is an independent creative studio operating catnbloom.com as a portfolio and showcase for original illustrated artwork. We are based in the United States. A correspondence address is available upon request through our Contact page.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Contact form submissions.** When you send a message through our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

**Newsletter subscriptions.** If you subscribe to receive updates, we collect your email address. Newsletter communications are managed through [—], a third-party email service provider. Your email address is stored and processed on their platform in accordance with their own privacy policy. By subscribing, you consent to receive occasional email updates about new artwork and studio news.

**Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery infrastructure may automatically log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and performance purposes.

**Cookies.** The Site may use minimal technical cookies necessary for basic functionality (e.g., remembering menu state). We do not currently use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Respond to messages sent through the Contact page
- Send newsletter updates to subscribers who opted in
- Maintain and improve the security and functionality of the Site

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our Site links to third-party marketplaces, including TeePublic and Redbubble, where our designs are printed and sold. When you visit or make a purchase through these platforms, your information is collected and processed by them under their own privacy policies. CatnBloom Studio does not have access to, and is not responsible for, data handling practices on these third-party platforms.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain contact form messages and newsletter subscriber information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, or until you request deletion.

## 6. Your Rights

**For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR).** You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us through our Contact page.

**For California residents (CCPA/CPRA).** You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of that information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. CatnBloom Studio does not sell personal information.

**Unsubscribing.** You may unsubscribe from newsletter emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in each email, or by contacting us directly.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
